# Skill visual audit

Survey only. Nothing here was fixed, and nothing in `js/` was touched.

Reproduce with `node tools/skill_visual_audit.mjs`; the full 420-row table is
`docs/skill-visual-audit.csv`, regenerated by `--csv`. The primitive→visual map
is sliced out of `js/compose.js` at run time rather than hand-written, so the
table re-derives itself instead of going stale.

---

## The answer, first

**The observation is real and neither of the two proposed causes explains it.**

95% of skill activations draw something. Skills fire 4.1 times a second. The
gap between "lots of skills firing" and "almost nothing on screen" is a **third**
cause: the visuals exist, render correctly, and are gone before a human can see
them.

> **A player projectile is on screen for a mean of 47 ms — 2.8 frames.**
> Ten of the fourteen classes sit at 17–27 ms, which is one to one-and-a-half
> frames at 60 fps.

A projectile is released the moment it touches a body. In an endgame room the
nearest body is a few tens of units away, so a 540 u/s bolt covers the distance
in two or three frames and vanishes. There is no flight to see. That is why
"projectiles" is the word the observation reached for — projectiles are 37% of
all activations and appear in **4% of frames**.

Adding art to these skills would not change this. They already have art.

---

## The two numbers

Region 2, representative node (depth > 1), level 60, every tree ranked to 4,
8 loadout slots, 30 s window after a 12 s warm-up, HP pinned. Mean over all 14
classes.

| | |
|---|---|
| **A — skill activations** | **4.1 per second** (122 per class per 30 s) |
| **B — player visuals on screen at any instant** | **1.2 elements** |
| … of which transient fx | 0.66 (arcs, beam flashes, rings) |
| … of which entities | 0.5 (projectiles, zones, minions, summons) |
| frames showing any player visual | 65% |
| frames showing a player **projectile** | **4%** |

**B is modelled against the renderer's own clocks**, not counted as fx pushes.
`sim.fx` is cleared every tick, so counting pushes would report "3 things this
frame" about effects a player sees for a sixth of a second. `js/render.js` holds
each ingested effect for a fixed duration — arcs and beam flashes 0.16 s, rings
0.25–0.35 s, floaters 0.8 s — and the measurement keeps those same durations.

### Do A and B disagree?

**Not in the way the brief expected, and the arithmetic is the point.** An
effect that lives 0.16 s, fired N times a second, sustains 0.16N of itself on
screen. At 4.1 fires/sec that predicts 0.65 concurrent transient effects;
measured, 0.66. The transient pipeline is behaving exactly as designed.

So the density ceiling is not a rendering bug — it is `fire rate × effect
lifetime`, and both terms are small. Nothing is dropped on the floor between
the sim and the screen except the items in the next section.

## Real rendering gaps found

These are genuine "effect exists, nothing renders" cases. They are small in
number and none of them accounts for the density observation.

### 1. `fx.skillFires` — every activation, never drawn

`js/skillsim.js:490` pushes one record per successful activation, carrying the
player index, position, skill id and domain. The comment above it reads:

> *"Broadcast: clients render the fire, they never originate it."*

`render.js` never reads the channel. It is written on every one of the 4.1
activations per second and consumed by nothing. This is the one piece of
plumbing that would let a fire be shown *as a fire* independent of what its
primitive happens to emit.

### 2. Traps are in no view at all — 5 skills

`addTrap` pushes to `sim.traps`. There is no `traps:` key in either view
assembly (`js/game.js`, `js/main.js`), no entry in `js/netcodec.js`, and no
render loop. A trap is placed, ticks, detonates and is never visible.

`asn_caltrops`, `asn_tripwire`, `asn_pressure_plate`, `monk_set_stone`,
`monk_second_stone`. 27 activations in the sample window — 2% of all fires.

### 3. `fx.telResolve` — written, never drawn

Pushed once per telegraph resolution in `js/telegraphs.js`. Not a player-skill
issue, noted because the audit walks the same channels.

## What this rules in and out

**Ruled out.** "Skills are firing but have no visual" is not the cause. 95% of
activations draw something, and the transient pipeline sustains exactly the
0.65 concurrent effects that 4.1 fires/sec at 0.16 s predicts.

**Ruled in, and it is a third thing.** Effect *lifetime* is the binding
constraint, and for projectiles it is not the declared lifetime but the impact
distance: 47 ms against a declared ttl of ~590 ms. A bolt in an empty room would
be visible for half a second; in the room it is designed for, it is visible for
two frames.

**Still open, and this audit does not answer it.** Whether 4.1 activations per
second is the intended rate. That is the power-curve question, and the number
above is the input it needs. If the intended rate is materially higher, the
density follows from it without any art being drawn.

**Three defects worth fixing on their own merits**, none of which moves the
density number much: traps are invisible (5 skills, 2% of fires), `skillFires`
is broadcast and never rendered, `telResolve` likewise.
